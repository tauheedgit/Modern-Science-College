Our Story

From humble beginnings to a respected educational institution

Building Futures, One Student at a Time

Modern Science College, Jogapur Pratapgarh was founded with a singular vision: to provide accessible, high-quality secondary and higher secondary education to students from classes 6 to 12. Over the years, we have grown into a full-fledged institution recognized for academic rigor and character formation.

Located in the culturally rich town of Bela Jogapur Pratapgarh, the College serves students from across the district and beyond. With a well-qualified teaching staff, modern classrooms, and a student-centric approach, we continue to set new benchmarks in education.

Our Journey

Key milestones in the history of Modern Science College

Foundation

Establishment of Modern Science College

Founded with the goal of bringing quality education to the Jogapur Pratapgarh region, the College began its journey with a handful of classes and a passionate teaching staff.

Growth

Expansion of Programs

Expanded to offer Science and Computer stream classes for classes 11-12 (Intermediate) to meet growing student demand and evolving educational needs.

Affiliation

Affiliated with the Government of Uttar Pradesh

Recognized by the Government of Uttar Pradesh, ensuring academic credibility and modern curriculum standards.

Present

Continuing the Legacy

Today, the College stands as a leading institution in the region, producing students who excel in board exams, competitive entrance exams, and professional careers.
