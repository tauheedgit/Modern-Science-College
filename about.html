About Modern Science College

A tradition of academic excellence, holistic growth, and community service since our establishment.

Our Story

From humble beginnings to a respected educational institution

Building Futures, One Student at a Time

Modern Science College, Pratapgarh was founded with a singular vision: to provide accessible, high-quality higher education to the youth of the region. Over the years, we have grown into a full-fledged institution recognized for academic rigor and character formation.

Located in the culturally rich town of Bela Pratapgarh, the college serves students from across the district and beyond. With a well-qualified faculty, modern infrastructure, and a student-centric approach, we continue to set new benchmarks in education.
