Eligibility Summary

Quick reference for admission requirements

📘 Class 6-8

  • Passed previous class from a recognized College
  • Basic proficiency in Mathematics & English preferred
  • Admission as per UP Board norms

  • Passed Class 8th from a recognized College
  • Minimum 40% aggregate
  • Admission as per UP Board (UPMSP) norms

  • Merit-based admission as per UP Board norms
